Mouse Reader

Read from here. Hold NVDA+control and click anywhere in a piece of text, and NVDA reads on from there until you press a key, the way Windows Magnifier's read-aloud does. The click is swallowed, so the app underneath does not see it: nothing gets selected or opened. Two commands skip back or forward a paragraph while it reads.

Reading uses NVDA's own Say All from the review cursor, so it stops on any key, scrolls the document, and follows NVDA's Say All settings. On a web page the position is carried into the page, so reading continues past the paragraph you clicked.

If nothing under the click has text at all (a picture of text, or an app such as Slack that cannot say what is at a screen point), and the option is on, Mouse Reader recognises the text in the window under the pointer with Windows' built-in OCR (the same engine as NVDA+R) and starts reading from the recognised paragraph nearest the click. Nothing opens and there is nothing to close: any key stops the reading, and the skip commands work. The window is asked to draw itself for the picture, so it works with Windows Magnifier in full-screen mode and covers the whole window, not only the part on screen.

For three minutes after that, the recognised text also answers the mouse: point at a spot in that window where NVDA itself finds no text and the recognised paragraph there is read, once per paragraph. Every NVDA+control+click recognises afresh, so a window that has scrolled is never read from an old picture; NVDA+control+shift+click recognises without reading. Recognised paragraphs are formed from the gaps between lines, column by column, so a sidebar and a message list stay apart; OCR gives plain text without links or headings, and takes a fraction of a second for a small window and a second or two for a large one.

Settings

The Mouse Reader category in NVDA menu > Preferences > Settings.

  • Read from here with NVDA+control+click: the click trigger on or off. The keyboard command works either way.
  • Read from here starts at: the beginning of the paragraph you clicked (the default), the beginning of the line, the beginning of the word, or the exact spot.
  • Use OCR when Read from here finds no text: on by default.

Commands

All of them can be changed in NVDA's Input Gestures dialog, under the Mouse Reader category.

  • NVDA+control+click: read from here. Where there is no real text it recognises the window first, then reads from the paragraph nearest the click.
  • NVDA+shift+R: read from wherever the mouse pointer is, without clicking.
  • NVDA+control+shift+click or NVDA+control+shift+R: recognise the window under the mouse without reading, for hovering.
  • Move the review cursor to the text under the mouse (no key by default): puts NVDA's review cursor there and says "Review cursor at mouse", so NVDA's own read-from-review command (NVDA+numpad plus, or NVDA+shift+A in the laptop layout) reads from the mouse.
  • NVDA+alt+comma and NVDA+alt+period: while reading, skip back or forward a paragraph and keep reading. After reading has stopped, they continue from the previous or next paragraph.
  • Any other key stops the reading, as with NVDA's own Say All; control on its own stops it too. Moving the mouse onto another recognised paragraph stops the reading and reads that paragraph; moving around inside the paragraph being read, or across blank space, does not interrupt it.

A delay for mouse tracking

Making NVDA's mouse tracking wait until the mouse has stopped is a separate add-on, Mouse Echo Delay, from the same author. The two work independently.
